## What the CEO sees

On 2026-08-09 a stronger and much dearer model became available on our subscription, the system moved the whole fleet onto it by itself, the usage ran out within hours and agents died in the middle of their work.

Two things protect the fleet now, and this proof shows the old way of changing a model is gone for good. The first is that a model choice has one home and only one: the Settings page offers only what the rules allow, nothing automatic climbs a list to a better model, and nothing can carry a choice around through a side door. The old route that sent a model change through a reviewed pull request has been removed, none was opened after it went, and a test in each repository fails the moment any copy of a choice comes back. The second is that the console keeps a history of every change with an undo, so a wrong choice is a few seconds to put right. The settings file that remains holds only facts that are not choices.
